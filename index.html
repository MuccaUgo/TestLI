High-frequency traps
Trap
Accrual ≠ cash

Revenue belongs to the year the transaction takes effect; the cash inflow can fall in a different year.

Trap
FVTPL ≠ Available-for-sale

Both are measured at fair value, but the variation goes to the income statement for FVTPL and to the revaluation reserve for available-for-sale.

Trap
Impairment ≠ depreciation

Depreciation allocates cost across useful life; impairment captures a durable, unrecoverable loss of value.

Trap
Land is never depreciated

Every other item of PPE is, and depreciation continues even while an asset sits idle.

Trap
LIFO is not allowed

IFRS permits FIFO and weighted average only; LIFO logic is considered unrealistic.

Trap
Inventories: lower of the two

Cost versus net realizable value, where NRV is the selling price less completion and selling costs.

Trap
Gross profit only by function

The by-nature format runs from revenue straight to operating profit with no gross profit subtotal.

Trap
Goodwill is tested, not amortized

Indefinite life means an impairment test every year, with the loss credited to the income statement.
